## 📖 What's Included

### ✅ Individual Farmer Registration
- Personal info (name, phone, email, ID, birth date, gender)
- Location (sub-county, ward with auto-population)
- Farm details (soil, size, water source, budget, crops, livestock)
- Profile photo upload (optional)
- Preferences (language, contact method)

### ✅ Farmer Group Registration
- Group information (name, registration number, description)
- Leader profile (with optional photo)
- Member management (add/remove unlimited members)
- Member details (name, phone, farm size)

### ✅ Login System
- Phone or email login
- Password authentication
- Remember me option

### ✅ Photo Upload
- Drag & drop support
- Real-time preview
- Format validation (JPG, PNG, GIF)
- Size validation (max 5MB)

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: How do I register a farmer?**  
A: Fill the individual farmer form on the "Individual Farmer" tab

**Q: How do I register a group?**  
A: Click "Farmer Group" tab, fill info, add members, submit

**Q: Can I upload photos?**  
A: Yes, for individual farmers and group leaders (JPG, PNG, GIF, max 5MB)

**Q: What happens after registration?**  
A: Farmer gets ID, data saved, redirects to farmer dashboard

**Q: Can I edit after registering?**  
A: Future feature, currently one-time registration

**Q: How are photos stored?**  
A: As Base64 encoded data in the database
