# 90-Second Hackathon Demo Script

## 0-15s: Problem
- Smallholder farmers in Siaya make crop decisions with fragmented weather, soil, and market information.
- Result: low yields, poor timing, weak market returns.

## 15-35s: Product Walkthrough
- Open Fahamu Shamba dashboard.
- Enable `Hackathon Demo Mode`.
- Click a demo persona (Bondo long rains / Gem short rains / Ugunja dry).
- System auto-fills profile and generates top-3 crop recommendations.

## 35-55s: Intelligence + Explainability
- Show top recommendation card with:
  - confidence score,
  - market price context,
  - explainability bars (`rule match`, `regional signal`, `feedback`, `market momentum`).
- Use `What-if Simulator` sliders (rainfall/market shocks) and apply preview.

## 55-75s: Planning + Impact
- Click `Build Season Plan`.
- Show timeline and scenario comparison (`Top 3 scenarios`).
- Highlight projected revenue, margin, and risk index.

## 75-90s: Trust + Scale
- Show model evaluation panel (Accuracy, F1, Top-3).
- Mention synthetic-data disclaimer and real-data roadmap.
- Close with expected impact: better crop selection, improved resilience, higher yields.
