How to Use:

  1. Click "Reset" to start fresh or dial *134*65#
  2. Select a language: 1=English, 2=Swahili, 3=Dholuo
  3. Choose service: 1=Crop advice, 2=Market prices, 3=Weather update, 4=About
  4. For crop advice: select sub-county, soil type, and season
  5. Click "Send" to continue at each menu step
